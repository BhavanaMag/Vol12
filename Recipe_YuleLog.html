Recipe: Yule Log

Over Christmas Break, when me and my family were in Florida, I decided to make a new recipe in celebration of Christmas. I searched online and found a recipe for a Buche de Noel, or Yule Log cake. The recipe required more ingredients than we had, so we needed to go shopping for some things like cocoa power and more sugar. The recipe requires two main parts: The cream filling, and the main sponge cake. The recipe required some things like having to make a whole sheet of the cake and then roll it up using parchment paper along with the cream, which was hard but fun to do. I ended up having a rather large yule log cake, which was nice. The recipe also gives instructions for decorations on the cake, but I didn't try them out, unfortunately. The recipe I used is below:
